The challenge

Roll out the same dashboards across Europe, with numbers that match the database everywhere.

The context

The proof by Tosca

Tosca can scan a web page, and therefore a Power BI report, to read its numbers. I turned it into an automatic comparison tool.

  1. Tosca opens the Power BI report and scans the page to read the KPI values.
  2. In parallel, the SQL query retrieves the same data from Google Cloud Platform.
  3. The two results are compared automatically.
  4. Regression tests are scheduled daily and weekly, with a report sent by email.

The same number on screen as in the database, before key users ever see it.

Test examples

Four concrete situations: what I do and what I check.

Testing a field or a KPI

What I do
I build the right SQL query on the data table.
What I check
That the value of the field or KPI is the one expected.

Testing a chart or a KPI page

What I do
I compare the numbers shown in Power BI with those in the database.
What I check
That form and substance match: what you see is what the database holds.

Regression: source vs production

What I do
With Tosca, I compare source data with production data.
What I check
That no change has altered an existing number.

Power BI vs Google Cloud Platform

What I do
Tosca scans the report page and reads the KPI values.
What I check
That they match the results obtained on the GCP side.
